# 🇮🇹 Our Italy — A Trip for Two

A little digital travel journal made for two. 💗

**Our Italy** is a cozy travel-planning website created around the idea of a couple exploring Italy together.
It combines a warm editorial-style design with a personal travel itinerary featuring **five beautiful places in Italy**.

Instead of feeling like a traditional travel website, the project is designed to feel like opening a **little magazine or scrapbook about a future trip for two**. ✈️🍋

## ✨ Features

* 🗺️ **5 Italian destinations** — Amalfi Coast, Venice, Tuscany, Cinque Terre and Lake Como
* 💕 Romantic travel-planning concept designed for couples
* 📖 Editorial / magazine-inspired layout
* ☀️ Detailed plans for each destination
* 🍋 Small travel moments, activities and date ideas
* 💌 Soft pastel visual style with playful decorative elements
* 📱 Responsive design
* ⚡ Smooth and interactive user experience

---

## 🎨 Design

The visual style was inspired by:

* romantic travel journals
* printed magazines
* postcards and scrapbooks
* soft pastel colors
* handwritten travel memories
* Italian summer aesthetics 🇮🇹

The goal was to make the website feel **personal and nostalgic**, rather than like a typical travel booking platform.

Every section tells a small part of the couple's future journey — from watching the sunset on the Amalfi Coast to wandering through the streets of Venice.

## 🌸 Why I Made This

I wanted to create something that felt more like a **memory than a website**.

The idea was to take a simple travel itinerary and turn it into something warm, playful and personal — like a little digital magazine created by one person for another.

It was also a fun opportunity to experiment with **editorial layouts, visual storytelling, typography, pastel UI and small decorative details**.
